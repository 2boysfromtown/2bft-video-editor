# Workflow — from a raw recording to finished videos

## 1. Watch (never edit blind)
1. `python -I scripts/new_project.py "<recording>" "<short path>\\<job>"` — copies the engine, probes the file, extracts 16 k + 48 k audio, transcribes (Whisper large-v3-turbo, CUDA, `--language` auto-checked on a 60 s sample: Hinglish streams transcribe best as `hi`; English words come back in Latin), maps energy, pulls a frame every 10 s into labelled contact sheets.
2. **Read every contact sheet** and the whole transcript — as packed text: `tools/packed_transcript.py work/transcript_hi.json work/packed.md` (one phrase per line with times; marks stretched words `~`, low-confidence words, suspected Whisper fill-ins `{HALLU?}` and long pauses). Grab full-size frames (`tools/grab.py`) at anything that matters; at a real decision point (which take, where exactly to cut, is that a laugh) render `tools/timeline_view.py` (filmstrip + waveform + words) instead of guessing. → `cutting.md` §1–2
3. Confirm the layout rects in `source.json` by measuring a full-size frame (our stream scene: screen 48,125 1412×794; webcam 1474,156 388×765; face ≈ y 400–760). If the scene differs, fix the rects and the face crops in `build.py` (PUNCH, camrects).
4. Find the **original files** of everything shown on screen (renders, episodes, images, reels) in your own project folders — originals beat screen-share copies. Sync clips to the stream with `tools/sync_clip.py` (audio cross-correlation; a pause in playback shows as an offset jump). Probe each one's colour (range, matrix, HDR) and normalise before use → `colour.md` §4.
5. **Session memory.** Keep a `## Session log` at the end of `EDIT-PLAN.md`: per session the strategy, decisions *with reasons*, a one-line reasoning trail, open items. On resuming a project, read it first and state the last session in one sentence before touching anything.

**The long-file transcript is a map, not the words.** Whisper silently translates stretches of Hinglish into English (and hid a client-pricing line as "4–5 videos ke liye"). Before captions or cut decisions that depend on exact wording — and always for money, numbers, names and claims — re-transcribe that short window (hi and en modes, `tools/lang_arbiter.py`). Run `tools/caption_audit.py` on every plan before rendering finals.

## 2. Understand (the log)
Write in `EDIT-PLAN.md`: what happens in order (with times); the strongest moments; dead stretches; **turns**; **irony pairs** (a confident claim before a failure — the best material in a stream); **proof frames** (outputs appearing); energy peaks (`tools/peaks.py`) checked against the transcript; what must be cut (income claims, private screens/logins, swears to bleep, third-party audio, garbled stretches). Log first-viewing reactions before you get used to the footage.

## 3. Research (every time)
Trending audio + rights (`music-trending-audio.md` rule 1), plus anything the footage needs (a format, a fact to verify). Ask once for downloads with name/source/size.

## 4. Director's pass (per deliverable) — `templates/director-pass.md`
What it is about emotionally · spine (one verb phrase) · packaging (title ≤ 60 chars, thumbnail 2–4 words; frame 0 continues the thumbnail) · template (A cold-open quest by default; B story circle; C clock challenge; D build log; E countdown) · beat list (want → obstacle → turn; beats with no turn get cut or compressed) · frame plot (tighter and faster as stakes rise, release wide on the payoff) · music map · where the one native move goes. Long-form: re-engagement at ~25 % and ~50 %, an open loop always running, payoff last then out in seconds, chapters valid (00:00, ≥ 3, each ≥ 10 s).
- **Make the plan auditable:** give every shot `beat` (HOOK / SETUP / TURN / PROOF / PAYOFF / CTA), `quote` (first … last words kept) and `why` keys — the engine ignores them, reviewers and the next session read them.
- **Decision log** (`review-qa.md` §9): for the track, the template and each Short pick, two options considered and the reason for the one chosen.
- **Sample first** when the style or format is new: render 10–15 s for real (the hook + one middle beat, with audio, captions and music) and get the user's yes before rendering everything — stills alone don't show timing.
- **Reference clip** (when the user points at a Short they like): measure its cut rate, speech rate, length and hook shape, use them as soft targets, and label each proposal "Inspired by: X · Twist: Y" so it never becomes a copy.

## 4b. Tighten (after the radio cut, before graphics) — `cutting.md`
For each kept range: `tools/pauses.py work/audio48k.wav work/transcript_hi.json <from> <to> --mode aggressive` (Shorts) / `balanced` (long-form) → threshold from the footage's own noise floor, the gap table, a cut list that skips cuts saving < 0.2 s, and lists of unworded voice (fillers, breaths, laughs), stretched words and retake candidates — none of which it cuts for you. Paste its `PLAN SHOTS` into the plan, then decide the flagged items by ear/eye. Protect planned beats before punchlines. Screen stretches: speed by activity (`cutting.md` §6).

## 5. Selecting Shorts from one stream
Self-contained test (no need for the previous 15–20 s), hook → context → development → payoff by 30–45 s. Score 1–5 on hook, self-containment, payoff clarity, emotional peak, visual proof, **flow** (it progresses and ends satisfyingly) and **trend fit**; pick under a mix quota (~4 demos/wow, ~4 lessons, ~3 fails/comedy, ~2 hot takes, ~2 mini stories). Reject clips that misrepresent him, need unseen context, duplicate another, or stop before the answer. Face medium-close (wide faces underperform).
- **Candidates:** list about **twice as many** as you need (≥ 30 for 15). Boundaries fall at sentence ends and topic shifts (read the packed transcript for where the subject changes), not at fixed windows. Treat two candidates as duplicates when their starts and ends differ by < 15 s in total, or they overlap > 50 % — keep the higher score. Write one `hook_sentence` per candidate.
- **Signals to combine:** energy peaks (`tools/peaks.py`), irony pairs, proof frames, and — when the stream's chat replay can be exported — chat spikes (messages per 10 s window several times the stream's baseline; start the candidate ~30 s before the spike because chat reacts late).
- **Batch variety (platform rule):** YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (Jul 2025) can demonetise a whole channel for mass-produced, templated videos an average viewer can't tell apart. Across the ~15 Shorts from one stream vary the hook format, the layout of the first 3 s, the structure and the music — the QUEST LAB look stays, the shape changes.
- **Instagram:** only the original of identical content is recommended; **visible watermarks make a Reel ineligible** — export Instagram files with no platform marks (the `*_clean.mp4` files). Reels may run to 3 min but non-follower reach favours ≤ ~90 s. Use **Trial Reels** (shown to non-followers only) to A/B two hooks. Check whether the account is creator or business before promising a trending song in-app (business accounts are reported to get only the Meta Sound Collection).
- **YouTube:** set each Short's **Related video** to the full episode in Studio, and end with a spoken + visual pointer in the last 5 s.

## 6. Build, render, review
- Plans in `plans/` (see `references/engine.md`, `templates/`). `python -I build.py plans/x.py --no-frames` for a quick check (duration, shot count, `shake_share`), then `python -I render.py plans/x.py --stills t1,t2,… --no-video` and **look at every still** before the full render.
- Full render: `python -I render.py plans/x.py` (Shorts: `--chunk 600 --jobs 1`, long-form `--chunk 2700 --jobs 1–2`). ~24 fps on the RTX 5070 Ti when the machine is quiet: a 7-minute video ≈ 10 minutes.
- **Render capacity (learned the hard way, 25 Oct 2026):** the GeForce card allows ~8 NVENC sessions and extra encodes *hang* rather than fail; each headless Chrome + ffmpeg chunk also takes ~1.5 GB RAM. With 3 parallel agents rendering + Unreal Editor + Blender open, the 32 GB PC hit 60/62 GB commit and every render stalled. `render.py` now takes a machine-wide slot (max 4 encodes, `QL_MAX_ENCODES`), times out and retries chunks, and reuses finished chunks. Before a big render, check `nvidia-smi --query-gpu=encoder.stats.sessionCount --format=csv` and free RAM; ask the user before closing their apps (never close them yourself). When parallelising with agents, give each agent `--jobs 1`.
- **Review loop → `review-qa.md`.** In short: `tools/qa_detect.py out/*.mp4 --strips review/qa` (black/frozen/silent stretches, loudness incl. max short-term, mono fold, empty first frame, WCAG flash test, static stretches, spec; cut list + cut strips at every cut) → contact sheet every 4 s (long) / 1 s (Short) + a 360 px phone sheet → `check_srt.py` with `--cuts` on every SRT → `brand_lint.py` on plans, notes and SRTs → a second-engine read-back of the final audio against the intended words (diff = junctions to fix: swallowed stutters, doubled words, half words) → notes as `t · where · problem → fix · keep` with BLOCKER / ISSUE / FYI → fix text, then timing, then detail → at most 2 rounds, then deliver with open items listed. Keep `_v1` when re-rendering. For big sets, a fresh reviewer sub-agent (`review-qa.md` §2).
- **Parallel agents:** brief each one with `templates/agent-brief.md` (one goal, absolute paths, exact specs, palette and fonts, the timeline, an anti-list, a deliverable checklist, "pick the obvious reading rather than asking"). Split only past ~6 deliverables — a second wave nearly doubles the time.

### Checklists (from `director-editor-mind.md` §8)
**Each cut:** emotion carries · new progressive information · cut on a completed thought, blink/breath or movement · subject within ~10 % of its previous position or the eye is led · not two shots of the same size in a row (the engine auto-punches consecutive face shots) · the reaction follows its true stimulus.
**Long-form:** first 30 s would work as a Short · re-engagement at ~25 % and ~50 % · nothing static > ~40 s · stakes stair-step · end-screen zone clear in the last 5–20 s.
**Effects:** every effect has a trigger · hit on the transient frame or one before · footage layer only · budget respected · biggest effect on the biggest moment · ≤ 3 flashes/s · text being read never shakes.
**Stream → video:** greetings, chat-name reading, logins and tech fiddling cut · waiting compressed (8–30× time-lapse with a chip and elevator/tick music, or a "later" card) · inside jokes explained or cut · stream music replaced.
**Shorts:** passes the context test · payoff by 30–45 s · frame 0 has a text hook in the safe box · captions in the safe box, clear of the right rail · layout switches on beats · loop seam checked · nothing claimable over 60 s.
**Engine bugs found and fixed on 25 Oct (watch the logs for them):** a time-lapse's last frame could ask for a source frame that was never extracted and the page stalled until timeout (now: frames extracted past each shot end, renderer falls back to the nearest frame and logs `WARN missing frame`); an inserted clip's audio that plays for < 50 % of the film got ~10,000× gain from a bad level estimate (now measured only where the clip plays, capped at +18 dB, and `mix.py` prints `WARNING bus … peak` if any bus explodes — never ship a mix whose log shows a peak far above 1).
**Found re-editing S02 on 27 Oct (check every plan for these):** a shot `out` placed from Whisper's word end chopped the last word ("ghum raha *hai*" — energy ran 0.25 s past it) → set outs from the waveform (`timeline_view.py`); a shot opened on a caption whose words were mostly before the `in` (1 s of near-silence under "Dekho bhai") → a caption must be heard inside its own shot; the music bed sat 31 dB under the voice because `mix.py` normalises across all cues and a loud drop elsewhere pulls a gentle bed down → read the new `music cue … dB under the voice` line and fix the cue's `gain` (target 18–24); a Whisper read-back of a mix with music garbled correct speech → read back the `_clean` mix, or short windows.
**Found on the EP 02 stream (27 Oct):** he stands up and leans — the fixed `vface='high'` crop showed only his shirt → face tracking (engine.md) is on by default now; a commercial song played on his screen (Devaralan Aattam, Bollywood) → map every song stretch with `sync_clip.py` against the original files, list them as "rights zones" in EDIT-PLAN, mute them and rebuild those moments from silent masters + licensed music; he paused the video to talk, so his lines sat in the gaps — check the waveform before assuming a line is lost; he said "cut this" on camera → honour it; he repeated a setup line ("then I got an idea") minutes apart → keep only the one next to its payoff; a long-file transcript turned some Hindi into English — `window_words.py` + `lang_arbiter.py` per caption window; `readmap.txt` (12 s windows, each in its own language) is a reliable map to direct from.
**Long-form captions are speech too (27 Oct, EP 02):** 15 burned-in captions in the YouTube cut were English *translations* ("now I can talk in 5 languages" for "abhi main 5 language mein baat kar sakta hoon", "I'm a new YouTuber" for "naya naya YouTuber hoon") because the plan was written from an English-mode transcript. Run `caption_audit.py` on EVERY plan before rendering, long-form included, and read the flags (Devanagari that is English spelled out = English, keep). Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish-Apex (Apache-2.0) writes Roman Hinglish directly and is a strong second opinion per caption window.
**Subtitles (SRT) for long-form, the method that worked:** pass 1 — word timings per spoken shot (faster-whisper on the source window, mapped to output time via the edit's audio json), cues split at 0.45 s pauses / 5.5 s / 70 chars; pass 2 — Roman Hinglish per cue with Hinglish-Apex; pass 3 — final text by hand (deletions only in the Hinglish track; an English translation track), S02-style inserted clips captioned from their own clock, ♪ cues for music-only inserts, merge/linger/condense to ~20 cps, then `check_srt.py --cuts <final cuts> --fix`.
**Phone clips instead of a stream (27 Oct, Day 2 reel from 3 iPhone clips in Drive):** Drive for Desktop's online-only files can fail mid-copy from Git Bash `cp` ("Invalid request code") or Explorer ("Insufficient system resources") — copy with PowerShell `Copy-Item`, retry from the "Copy of …" duplicate, and check byte sizes against Drive. Join the clips into one 30 fps 1080×1920 source (`fps=30,scale`, libx264 crf 12, pcm, then concat) and write each clip's start into `source.json`'s `layout_note`; run `new_project.py --no-facetrack`, then `tools/face_track.py --cam 0,0,1080,1920`, and set `screen`/`cam`/`face` to the full portrait frame. Selfie shots use layout `v_cam` (full-bleed, face-tracked; crop ~1.13× with the eyes at 40 % so chest captions clear the chin), `shake=0` (handheld already), and a measured `cam_grade` for night footage (face luma 52 → `bright=1.75`). A phone filming his monitor: sync it to the original file by cross-correlating the frame-luma derivative (beat edit: r 0.905), push into the screen and match-cut to the original on the same frame. Mute the phone's room audio when a song is playing. A busy chat that scrolls past in a blur: hold its sharpest readable frame as an `img:` asset under his voice. Captions: a comma right after a `{sticker}` renders with a gap ("workflow ,") — leave it out.
**Pitfalls seen on this channel:** level cards drawn under captions (now top layer); HUD outliving its shot onto a face card; captions revealing word-by-word too slowly in long-form (now fast cascade); a still image held > 6 s with nothing new; the screen showing unreadable chat text while he talks — use the FACE layout then.

## 6b. Packaging (part of the job, not an extra)
- **Thumbnails:** two or three genuinely different concepts per long-form video (A = the hook, B = the honest payoff, optional C), each ≥ 1280×720 (smaller drops every variant to 480p in the test), 1280×720 JPG < 2 MB, in the sticker system: blue dot-grid world, tilted cards with ink outline + hard shadow, 2–4 huge words in Bahnschrift Bold Condensed stickers (mint = the want, red = the fail, yellow = numbers/loot). Find the face with `tools/face_candidates.py <times…>` on energy peaks (laughs read better than "shock" he didn't do — never fake a reaction). Pick the clearest object for the idea (one gold cube beat four tiny white boxes). Test at 320×180 — every word must read. Template: `tools/thumbs_example.py`. YouTube *Test & compare* takes up to 3 variants of thumbnail, title, or both, and picks on watch-time share — offer title variants too (`review-qa.md` §10).
- **Captions:** transcribe the *final* audio (`tools/transcribe_words.py`), then write `<name>.hi-Latn.srt` (Roman Hinglish, cleaned of fillers, never added words) and `<name>.en.srt` (same timings). Netflix-style timing (`review-qa.md` §4): 0.83–7 s cues, ≤ 42 chars a line, ≤ 2 lines bottom-heavy, ≤ 20 cps, gaps 2 frames or ≥ 0.5 s, in/out snapped to cuts, no punch-word spoilers, nothing over music-only stretches. Run `tools/check_srt.py <dur> <srt> --cuts review/qa/<name>.cuts.txt --fix` and re-check the `.fixed.srt`. Use the plan's `caps` as the spelling reference.
- **Shorts hooks:** run `tools/hook_audit.py` — every hook's top edge ≥ ~200 px (status bar + Reels/search icon row), lines ≤ ~830 px wide.

## 7. Deliver
`exports/youtube/*.mp4`, `exports/shorts/*.mp4` + `*_clean.mp4` + notes (title, description, hashtags, trending song to add in-app and where, credit lines, chapters for long-form, what to check by ear), `CREDITS` (footage owner, original files used, music + credit lines, fonts: Inter & JetBrains Mono OFL, Bahnschrift Windows system font, SFX synthesised in code, transcription local Whisper). Tell the user what only a human can judge: the first 5 seconds, the turn, the ending — on phone speakers and headphones. Run `tools/brand_lint.py` on the plans, notes and SRTs last and read every flag. When the footage fought the edit (tiny text, small webcam, no cursor data), add **next-stream setup notes** (`cutting.md` §9). After publishing: the retention check, Studio trim and Test & Compare steps in `review-qa.md` §10.
